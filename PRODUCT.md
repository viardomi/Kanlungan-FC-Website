# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: greenfield React + Vite frontend, chosen for a lightweight multi-view marketing and registration experience that can later connect to a CMS or backend.

## Users

Parents and guardians in Consolacion and nearby Cebu communities looking for a welcoming football pathway for children ages U7–U15. Current families need schedules, results, announcements, and media in one reliable place. Potential sponsors and partners need an official point of contact.

## Product Purpose

An official online home for Kanlungan Football Club that recruits new players, supports current families, showcases youth development, shares confirmed achievements, and opens a clear channel for registration and partnership inquiries.

## Positioning

Kanlungan is a community-first football club where progress, belonging, and consistent development matter as much as competition.

## Operating Context

Weekend training is held at Genesis Valley or Pino Football Pitch depending on availability. The site must help families find the right age group, understand the programs, check club updates, and contact the club without needing to search social media first.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Required content areas: Home, About, Programs, Teams, Coaches, Achievements, News and Announcements, Gallery, Sponsors, and Contact / Register.
- Team age groups: U7 through U15.
- Confirmed achievements: CarCar Mayors Cup 2024 — U7 Champions; Bogo City FC 1st Grassroots Community Football Tournament — U7 Champions; Sandugo Football Festival — First Runner-Up.
- Registration is frontend-only until an email service or backend is connected. The UI must state this integration point honestly.
- Do not publish fees, exact training times, coach names, player names, rosters, or unconfirmed tournament details.
- Use “Coach” as the staff label until official names are available.
- Map directions must be available for Genesis Valley and Pino Football Pitch.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied the club facts and confirmed achievement text. The official crest asset is stored at `public/KanlunganCrest.svg`. The official Facebook profile is the source for public club photos and videos, but public image URLs could not be reliably fetched during implementation; the media wall therefore links to Facebook and avoids attributing unrelated imagery to the club.

## Product Principles

- Make the first step into the club feel easy and human.
- Treat development and belonging as visible outcomes, not abstract claims.
- Keep every update grounded in confirmed information.
- Make weekend logistics easy to scan on a phone.
- Give families and partners a direct, respectful way to reach the club.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, high-contrast black/yellow combinations, descriptive alt text, responsive layouts, and clear validation/error/success states.
